THEN

Album

Her Social Anxiety (Album)

17 July 2025

Her Social Anxiety (album) ; Solitude Is Bliss ; 2015

What are you supposed to do while you are anxious?

Often enough we run into all sorts of problems, and when those problems pass, we tend to come away carrying something with us — something that can never be erased, even once the problem is over.

This album seems to take us through the journey of a woman who has been through love, desire, dreams, and the exploration of her own self.

For this album I would like to talk about the songs I love personally, and that I would like everyone to go and listen to.

Venting (tracks 1–2):

Every time we are drunk, even as the picture of the real world slowly blurs, memory grows only sharper. And when the racing of the mind is carried in along with the alcohol, music becomes the only way out.

Listening to ‘ระบายกับเสียงเพรียก’ cannot help but bring to mind ‘Shine On You Crazy Diamond’ by Pink Floyd — in the placement of the instrumental and vocal sections, in the length of the song, and with the familiar synth on top. It should have no trouble holding a place in the hearts of progressive rock listeners.

Desire (tracks 3–5):

Personally I think this stretch is especially melancholy, whether from the perspective of ‘สตรี’ or from the longing of ‘Sleep Tight (03.00 A.M.)’.

“She left everything, she gave up everything, for you”

‘สตรี’ tells the story of a woman and a young man — a man so lost in the lust and desire around him that he forgets that the one who loves him had also once given up her own dreams, no differently from him. It is another song whose meaning is straightforward yet cuts rather deep.

“The scent that runs past, along the path of the nostrils, wakes me into dying out of what is real”

‘Sleep Tight (03.00 A.M.)’ takes us to the boundary between dream and reality, between memory and delirium. A familiar scent alone, or a small fragment of an image in memory, is enough to make us long for something that might once have been ours — as though it were right there at our feet.

Selfhood (tracks 6–11):

“She was born, how many years have passed, what is it that is good?”

‘Wanderer Land’ is like a wanderer’s song — one who thinks, and wonders, and loses his way in his own actions. If you are the sort who drifts, waiting on fate to come, the thing that can never be given back may be the time you lost.

It is a song strangely well suited to anyone lost in life, and I believe it must push its listeners forward at least a little.

There is no denying this is one of the albums that speaks most about the search for the self — the confusion, the change, the restless mind, and the freedom. All of it laid end to end, a little at a time, a road toward a heart that is hard to fathom.

‘ใหม่ซ้ำเดิม’ may be one of my favourite songs on the album. No showy effects, nothing to nod your head to — but a song whose content is good enough that we can sit with our own lives for a while.

Loving something, letting something go — the story of one’s life right now seems, somehow, oddly familiar. Considered carefully, a great many things are new all over again, waiting for us to keep on learning them.

That is everything I have to say about this album — and there is truly no denying that 'Solitude Is Bliss' is one of the bands that strings words together most beautifully. Both in the words they choose and in the stories they choose to tell, they take us exploring a world of music without end.
