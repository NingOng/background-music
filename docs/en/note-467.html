忘記他 (Forget Him)

25 May 2025

“In a few days a woman will come asking for me. Give her this coin, and tell her that ‘1818’ is my lucky number.”

“ ’1818’ is a code on the jukebox. When she plays it, she’ll understand what it means.”

“…ลืมเขาเสีย ต่างไฉนกับลืมทุกสิ่ง
สูญสิ้นไร้ทิศทาง เลือนรางร้างตัวตน...”

“忘記他” (Forget Him) is a lonely song that is hard to forget — but before it arrived at being a soundtrack in Wong Kar-wai’s film ‘Fallen Angels’,

it was a song written for Taiwan’s immortal legend among female singers, Teresa Teng.

Teresa Teng was one of those singers renowned for singing in a great many languages: Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Cantonese from Hong Kong, and even Japanese, English, and others.

“忘記他” appeared on her first Cantonese-language album, in 1980. One thing that makes the song special is that it was influenced by the Japanese genre of enka.

Enka carries many meanings as a genre, but for this article it means a kind of ballad — that is, a slow song, with a melody tending toward sadness, that reaches the listener’s emotions. It was a genre that grew popular after the Second World War.

Enka uses the pentatonic scale, and often features the sound of the mandolin and of bowed strings such as the violin.

It was this influence from enka that made the song rather different from other Hong Kong songs of the period.

The other thing is that Teresa Teng’s image was of someone poised, polite, and always smiling — yet this is one of the few songs of hers that expresses the anguish, the restraint, and the loneliness that come with love.

Fifteen years later, Shirley Kwan recorded the song again, with a dream-pop interpretation — that is, a hazy feeling, with vocals, harmonies, and music that seem to have escaped from a dream.

Shirley’s “忘記他” was used in Wong Kar-wai’s film ‘Fallen Angels’ (1995).

In the scene, it tells of a pair of partners in killing who have worked together a long time, but who cannot meet for certain reasons. When the final job is done, she has to forget him — to forget that each of them exists, even though they have never met at all.

จากสามัญธรรมดาสู่ความสวยงาม ลืมเขา... ฉันจะทำได้อย่างไร”
